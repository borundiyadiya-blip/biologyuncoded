Cancer cells bypass normal limits on replication by hijacking the enzyme **telomerase** to sustain their telomeres — protective caps at the ends of chromosomes that normally shorten with age. This telomerase activity is a hallmark of 80 %+ of cancers and helps tumors evade cell death. ([MAIA Biotechnology, Inc.](https://ir.maiabiotech.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/163/maias-ateganosine-surges-ahead-with-breakthrough?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

**Maia Biotechnology** has built its strategy around this fundamental vulnerability, developing **ateganosine (also called THIO or 6-thio-2′-deoxyguanosine)** — a first-in-class small molecule designed to exploit telomerase’s presence in cancer cells while sparing healthy tissues. ([MAIA Biotechnology, Inc.](https://ir.maiabiotech.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/163/maias-ateganosine-surges-ahead-with-breakthrough?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Traditional chemotherapies and targeted therapies attack cancer indirectly; **ateganosine directly targets telomeres** by being incorporated into telomeric DNA. Once inserted, it disrupts telomere structure, triggers DNA damage responses, and induces selective cancer cell death. ([MAIA Biotechnology, Inc.](https://ir.maiabiotech.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/138/maia-biotechnology-announces-positive-efficacy-update-for?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## Mechanism Meets Immune Activation

Ateganosine’s _dual mechanism_ — direct telomere disruption plus immune system engagement — reflects a new paradigm in oncology where **DNA damage and immunobiology intersect**. By disrupting telomeres within cells that rely on telomerase, the drug not only halts proliferation but also creates signals that can activate immune pathways like **cGAS/STING**, potentially increasing tumor visibility to the immune system. ([MAIA Biotechnology, Inc.](https://ir.maiabiotech.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/163/maias-ateganosine-surges-ahead-with-breakthrough?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

This strategy is distinct from classic targeted drugs (which require specific mutations) or from immunotherapies alone (which aim to unleash T cells). It represents a **hybrid approach** where genome integrity and immunity converge.
